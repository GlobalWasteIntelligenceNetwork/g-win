About G-WIN

A global movement transforming waste into climate action.

Founder

Yani Ma Zhang

Yani Ma Zhang is a high school student who created the Global Waste Intelligence Network to help communities measure and reduce their waste footprint. G-WIN is the first youth-led global waste dataset, aiming to create measurable reductions in landfill waste worldwide.
