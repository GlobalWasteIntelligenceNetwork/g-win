About G-WIN

Building the first global youth-led waste intelligence network.

Founder

Yani Ma Zhang

Yani Ma Zhang is a Fellow from Climate Leaders Fellowship (Rustic Pathways x Stanford Democracy Lab) who created the Global Waste Intelligence Network to help communities measure and reduce their waste footprint. G-WIN is the first youth-led global waste dataset, aiming to create measurable reductions in landfill waste worldwide.
