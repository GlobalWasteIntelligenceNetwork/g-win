How G-WIN Works

Our platform helps communities measure, analyze, and reduce waste using data-driven solutions.

1. Measure

Communities conduct a simple audit of landfill, recycling, and compost waste.

2. Upload

Data is securely uploaded to the G-WIN platform.

3. Analyze

Advanced analytics identify waste patterns and reduction strategies.

4. Act

Communities implement data-backed initiatives to reduce waste.
