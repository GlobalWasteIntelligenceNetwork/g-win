Turning Waste Into Intelligence

The Global Waste Intelligence Network (G-WIN) transforms how communities understand waste. Instead of focusing only on recycling awareness, the platform collects real data from students, schools, and organizations worldwide. This website serves as a standardized, easy-to-use waste audit protocol for schools and communities; live website with dashboards showing landfill, recycling, and compost data; predictive tool recommending high-impact interventions; and open dataset for research and policy guidance.

By mapping waste patterns globally, G-WIN empowers communities to identify the most impactful solutions to reduce landfill waste and drive measurable environmental change.
